ORDER INFORMATION

Customers can check their order status using the order tracking option available in their NovaShop account.

After an order is placed, customers receive an order confirmation through email.

If an order has not been shipped, customers may contact customer support to request a cancellation.


SHIPPING POLICY

Standard shipping usually takes 5 to 7 business days.

Express shipping usually takes 2 to 3 business days.

Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location and unexpected delivery delays.

Customers receive tracking information after their order has been shipped.


CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers can request cancellation before the order has been shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.

Customers should contact customer support as soon as possible if they want to cancel an order.


REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of receiving the product.

The product should be unused and returned in its original condition.

Some products may not be eligible for a refund.

Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product has been received and inspected.


RETURN POLICY

Customers can request a return within 30 days of receiving an eligible product.

Products must generally be unused and in their original condition.

Customers should contact customer support before sending a product back.

Return shipping conditions may depend on the reason for the return and the product category.


PAYMENT METHODS

NovaShop accepts the following payment methods:

1. Credit cards
2. Debit cards
3. UPI
4. Net banking
5. Selected digital wallets

Payment must be successfully completed before an order can be processed.


DAMAGED OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS

If a customer receives a damaged or incorrect product, they should contact customer support as soon as possible.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs of the damaged or incorrect product.

The support team will review the request and provide instructions for the next steps.


ACCOUNT INFORMATION

Customers can update their profile information after logging into their NovaShop account.

Customers should never share their password or payment details with customer support.

If a customer forgets their password, they can use the password-reset option on the login page.
